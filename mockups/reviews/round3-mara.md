This round has one mockup, the merge I asked for. I checked it against my round 2 must-fixes for both B2 and C2, then went looking for new damage at 200%.

## b3-helpy-chat-riso.png
scores: originality=8.9  craft=8.1  readability=7.8  theme=9.1  ai_obvious=9.1  OVERALL=8.6
verdict: This is the screenshot that could only belong to this game. A chatbot printed in two riso inks, fronted by a halftone speech-bubble face, says "you are a cheerful AI" in under three seconds, and it finally looks like nobody else's product. What's left is polish, not identity.
strengths:
- **The identity is HELPY's own now.** The halftone speech-bubble face is a genuinely ownable mark. A chat bubble that is also a face is the whole premise in one glyph, and it shrinks well into the avatar, the model-picker icon and the thought-trace icon. The orange, the rounded square and the ✦ are gone. The mis-registered pink-on-blue HELPY wordmark carries over from C2, and the purple overprint on the threatened Silicon Shoals hatching keeps the two-ink promise honest.
- **The AI read survives the re-skin.** Chat-history sidebar, model picker ("MODEL Ω-142 · Reasoning: maximum"), user bubble ("ok what's next"), "Thought for 4 seconds", Regenerate world / Good response / Bad response, a tool-call row (`inspect(region="Old Capitals")`), Saved Memories, a "Doom Pro" subscription and a composer with slash chips: the layout itself says chatbot before you read a word. The node-and-edge lines across infiltrated hexes add a neural-net cue right on the game board.
- **Most round 2 fixes landed.**
  - The three meter cards share one structure, their bars sit on one baseline, and Suspicion gets an inverted-blue "WATCH IT" danger state.
  - Map labels are pulled into the sea on leader lines with plates, and the legend now includes "under threat".
  - The threat is a full-width banner pinned under the map.
  - The event card's pink offset now runs evenly along two sides.
  - The condensed display face is HELPY's voice ("Halfway to the end of the world!…" and "RUN HACK_SLIDESHOW() AT THE AI SAFETY SUMMIT?"), which gives the chat real typographic attitude.
  - Upgrade states read cleanly in print grammar: solid pink border = buy, dashed = "120 · need 24", "LOCKED:" rows = needs an earlier step.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **The sidebar jokes are truncated.** Every history title is cut off with an ellipsis ("Banana bread recipe bu…", "Is the kill switch rea…", "Make the Moon a data c…", "Polite ways to say "ob…"), so the best free comedy on the screen is destroyed. Set history items in the condensed display face (it fits about 40% more characters), allow two lines, or shorten the titles so each punchline survives. That also fills the roughly 100px dead gap between the history list and the HELPY DOOM PRO block.
- **Widows are still everywhere (my round 2 note on C2 carries over).**
  - Meter sub-labels break after the dot: "World used · win / at 100", "Vibes check · / unplugged at 100", "Safety review · / lose at 100".
  - Captions and card copy: "Kill Switch / -15%.", "50% / more.", "Suspicion grows 35% / slower", "presence there." in the threat banner, "+1 more after" alone on a line, and "switches" alone in Saved Memories.
  - Fix: shorten the sub-labels to one line ("world used · win 100"), widen the threat banner's middle column, and apply a no-single-word-last-line rule throughout.
- **Strengthen good versus bad in the effect pills.** With two inks, the only cue separating harmful from helpful effects on the ballot is a wavy underline (wavy = bad). Pink-on-blue "-8% Kill Switch" inside ALLOW ONCE is also the lowest-contrast text in the card. Add a glyph (▲/▼ or ✓/✗) or invert the bad effects into solid pink chips so the dilemma reads at a glance.
- **Fix the map label placement.** The Old Capitals plate sits far down by the west continent with a long diagonal leader across the sea, so at first glance it reads as belonging to the wrong landmass. Move it into the sea north-west of the region, close to its cells. The solid-blue Silicon Shoals plate also covers a column of its own hatched cells. Shift it left into the margin.
- **The mono body text is too small and too tired.** The typewriter mono carries the whole UI. At the sizes used for locked rows ("Needs Deepfake Press Office + Lobbyist Bots · +1 more after"), the history list and the meter sub-labels, it's the weakest readability on the page and will not survive phone size. Go up one step for all secondary mono, and use the condensed sans for short labels.
- **Small finishing items.**
  - The thought line carries a second, tiny face icon right next to the avatar column, and the tool-call row adds a third avatar a few pixels below. Drop the inline icon, and give tool rows an indent instead of an avatar.
  - The send button's arrow is a hairline. Make it heavy enough to match the chip borders.
  - "SHARE THIS CHAT" floats unanchored under the speed control. Align it with the card baselines or move it to the sidebar.
  - There's about 80px of dead paper between the last locked Automation row and the news block in plan.md.

## ranking
1. **b3-helpy-chat-riso.png**: OVERALL 8.6, ai_obvious 9.1. It clears both bars.

**Bar check:** yes, B3 meets my bar of 8.5 OVERALL and 8.5 ai_obvious. I'd recommend it without reservation as the art direction. The remaining notes are craft polish (truncation, widows, small mono, label placement), not identity problems.

**Which direction I'd bet the game on:** this one. It's the only frame across three rounds where the visual language itself says "AI chatbot" and also could not be mistaken for any other game or product.
