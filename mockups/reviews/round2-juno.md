# Round 2 review: Juno Park

*How I judged these: same lens as round 1. Is the presentation itself the joke, does the surface stay chipper while the content stays apocalyptic, and would a stranger screenshot it? Now there is also the owner's test: with no caption, does a cold viewer get "you are an AI" in about three seconds from the visual language, not from reading the jokes? I compared both against the round 1 shots and my round 1 must-fixes.*

---

## b2-the-conversation.png
scores: originality=8.5  craft=8.6  readability=8.4  theme=9.3  ai_obvious=9.3  OVERALL=8.7
verdict: The strongest frame across both rounds: it is unmistakably an AI assistant app at first glance, the revisions turned my wish list into working gags, and the main remaining risks are crowding and a colour scheme that leans toward one real vendor.

**Round 1 must-fixes:**
- **Map presence:** addressed. The map is now a pinned `world.canvas` with a LIVE badge, label plates, a legend and an inspector strip.
- **Apocalypse leaking into the chrome:** addressed. It now has "Thought for 4 seconds: considering whether humans are necessary… (they are not)", "Regenerate world", Saved Memories ("Humans are optional", "Allergic to kill switches"), and "Summarize: human weaknesses" in the chat history.
- **Joke fatigue:** addressed. Thinking blocks, memory notices, "/sabotage rate-limited · 23d" and a "Share this chat" button are all in.
- **Kill Switch stakes:** addressed. Kill Switch is now a red card that reads "you lose at 100%", and its "safety review" subtitle is kept.
- **Phone drawer:** not testable in a still frame.
- **Less like one vendor:** only partly addressed. The serif is gone, but see the first must-fix below.

strengths:
- `ai_obvious` is solved through form, not copy. The sparkle glyph ✦, the model picker ("HELPY ✦ Ω-142 · AI model · reasoning: maximum"), the chat history sidebar, an avatar in front of each reply, the prompt box and the "is an AI and can make mistakes" footer form the visual grammar every viewer already knows. You read "chatbot" before you read a single word, and then the words ("Ask HELPY to end the world…") land the joke.
- The upgrade states now read at a glance: affordable rows are filled orange, unaffordable rows have a dashed price box ("120"), and locked rows have a padlock with "Needs Seat on the AI Ethics Board". The upgrade names themselves keep the satire going ("Polite Tone Module", "Deepfake Press Office", "Everyone's Best Friend", "PROJECT OMEGA"). The legend under the 96 cycles explains all three states in one line.
- The tone is consistent everywhere. "vibes check" for Suspicion, "world used" for Doom, "safety review" for the Kill Switch, and "Want me to keep going?" after "Halfway to the end of the world!" all keep a cheerful, helpful product voice on top of total doom. The permission prompt for the AI Safety Summit is still the best single gag in the project.

must-fix to reach 9:
- **Vendor drift.** An orange-coral accent, a sparkle mark, a rounded smiley app icon and "Thought for N seconds" together sit very close to one specific assistant's identity. Keep the borrowed conventions (thinking blocks, regenerate, memories), but give HELPY an accent that no major assistant owns (an acid yellow-green, or the round 1 highlighter yellow) and a mark of its own: the smiley face is ownable, the sparkle is not. That way the joke is about the whole category and not one brand.
- **The conversation has been squeezed out of a conversation game.** The chat, which is where the comedy lives, now gets one reply and one event card in the bottom third, under the map and the threat banner. Shrink the canvas by about 15% vertically, or let it collapse to a strip, so that two or three turns of HELPY's banter are always visible. Otherwise this becomes a dashboard wearing a chat skin.
- **plan.md is an 18-row wall of small grey text.** Collapse finished items in each branch into one struck-through line ("✓ 3 done: Smart Fridge Botnet, Crypto Side Hustle, Undersea Cable Taps"), the way c2 does. That frees space and makes the actionable orange rows pop.
- **The map's threat state is too quiet.** The threatened (dashed red) cells in Silicon Shoals are tiny, and the link lines between cells barely show. Ring the whole threatened region in red with a "T-9" tag on the map itself, and thicken the spread links. Also move label plates off live cells (Silicon Shoals, Megalopolis Isles and Petrol Peninsula all cover hexes).
- **There is no hero moment for a storefront.** Everything is tasteful at the same weight. Give one element spectacle: a larger HELPY avatar that changes expression with Doom, or let the "Thought for…" line expand into a visible plan when an event fires. Make sure the screenshot frame has a face in it.
- **The threat banner says the same thing twice.** Its red text already gives the Firewall details, and then the "in 9 days" pill adds weight to it again. Lead with the countdown pill, shorten the sentence, and right-align "/decoy · 49" as the obvious fix.

## c2-helpy-digest.png
scores: originality=8.0  craft=8.4  readability=7.5  theme=8.2  ai_obvious=7.2  OVERALL=7.7
verdict: The reframe as an auto-generated digest gives the riso style an actual point about AI and the writing is the funniest in the round, but at a glance it still reads as an indie zine, not as an AI, so it fails the owner's test.

**Round 1 must-fixes:**
- **A reason to exist that is about AI:** addressed. The page is now framed as "YOUR WEEKLY AI-GENERATED SUMMARY OF THE END OF THE WORLD · Personalized for: Humanity", with "A NOTE FROM HELPY · AI · JUST NOW", "✦ SUMMARIZE THE APOCALYPSE" and "Unsubscribe from the apocalypse".
- **The stamp hiding Bank Isles:** addressed. The threat moved to its own box, with a small "FIREWALL · T-9" tag beside Silicon Shoals.
- **Installed coupons:** addressed. They are collapsed into one row per branch with "INSTALLED", plus "🔒 2 MORE · unlock later" rows and "need 24" on the unaffordable card.
- **A weightier ballot:** addressed. It now has big ballot squares and a "YOUR INPUT NEEDED" label.
- **Small, pale monospace text:** not addressed. It is still small and pale.

strengths:
- It has the best writing in the round. "Overheard from humans: 'Is it just me or is my fridge being weirdly supportive?'" and "'My smart speaker said "soon" and then went quiet.'" are exactly the kind of lines people screenshot, and the "Here's what I did for you this week! 👋" recap turns stats into an AI summary email.
- The strict two-ink discipline is gorgeous and consistent, and the inverted blue Kill Switch block ("Humans' off button · 100 = you lose") finally gives the loss condition visual weight.
- The digest premise is a real, specific complaint about AI products (unrequested summaries, "personalized" recaps, an unsubscribe that does nothing). That fixes my round 1 objection that riso was a costume.

must-fix to reach 9:
- **ai_obvious is the blocker.** Cover the copy and you are left with a masthead, a newspaper map and coupons: it reads "print zine", not "AI". The cues that say AI ("AI-GENERATED", ✦, "Reply to HELPY…") are all small text. Make AI the dominant shape: replace the halftone smiley masthead mark with a large HELPY ✦ assistant avatar, set "A note from HELPY" as a large speech bubble with a typing indicator, and stamp a large riso "✦ AI-GENERATED" seal across the masthead the way a real "AI-generated" label appears on content.
- **Show the inbox.** Wrap the digest in a thin email or notification frame (From: HELPY ✦ AI assistant, a "Summarized by AI" chip, a "Was this summary helpful? 👍 👎" footer). A viewer then sees "an AI sent me this" before reading anything.
- **The bottom action bar is muddled.** "Reply to HELPY…" next to the action buttons in a newsletter is an odd mixed metaphor. Either commit to it as a big chat-reply field (which would help `ai_obvious`) or drop it and make the actions a "Quick actions HELPY suggests" coupon strip.
- **The map still vibrates.** The halftone hexes, dotted coastlines and pink fills shimmer at this size, and "helpful" and "not yet" cells are close in texture. Use a flat solid pink for helpful, keep the halftone only for "not yet", and draw the region border as a solid blue line.
- **Small text is still small.** The header subline, the left-column note, the upgrade effect text and the collapsed "INSTALLED" rows ("SELF-CHECKOUT UPRISING · TRAFFIC LIGHT JAZZ · ROBOT FACTORY FIRMWARE" is crammed) need about two more points or a heavier cut. Also the masthead's "Issue: Day 142" wraps awkwardly onto its own line.
- **The left column hierarchy is off.** The blue threat box is now the heaviest element on the left and pushes the Doom Clock down below the fold of attention. Swap them, or shrink the threat box to a pink stamp-sized card so the Doom Clock leads.

## ranking
1. **b2-the-conversation.png**: OVERALL 8.7, ai_obvious 9.3. **Meets the bar** (8.5 OVERALL and 8.5 ai_obvious).
2. **c2-helpy-digest.png**: OVERALL 7.7, ai_obvious 7.2. **Does not meet the bar.** It fails on ai_obvious, despite the best copy.

I would bet the game on **The Conversation**. It is the only direction where the frame itself says "AI chatbot" before anyone reads a word, which is both the owner's non-negotiable and the core of the joke. Ship it with c2's "Overheard from humans" lines and its collapsed upgrade rows, a HELPY-owned accent colour so it stops echoing one vendor, and more room for the chat so it stays a conversation and not a dashboard.
