# Round 6 review: Sam Reyes (player voice)

I judged these from the stills and filmstrips only, using the brief's description of what animates between frames. Both prototypes share one HUD: a red-eye HELPY logo, three meters, cycles and day, an 18-tile upgrade grid, an action bar with a prompt line, and a news line. Both look comfortably under 60 words at rest. I counted by eye and didn't run the script.

---

## e1-nightside (globe), day 60 / 220 / 300

**5-second gut:** Yes, and I'd show a friend the day-300 frame. It's a dark planet in space with amber city lights, and red machine cubes eat it cell by cell. By day 300 the whole atmosphere glows red. That's Plague Inc's best feeling (watching the world go your colour) with an evil-AI coat of paint. The cyan geodesic dome at day 220 is the best single image in the round: I instantly get "humans are shielding that spot from me". The red eye in the corner is HAL 9000. Nobody needs a caption to know an AI is doing this.

**What I'd do first / click:** I'd watch the red spread and click the bright upgrade tiles. When the dome showed up, I'd look for something to press against it. I'd probably try the duck in the action bar, because I remember it from round 5. I don't think a new player would.

**What confuses me:**
- **Where am I on the map?** There are no region names on the globe. Half the world is on the back of the sphere, and the regions on the rim are squashed into slivers (the right edge at days 220 and 300). The Kill Switch at Research Ridge is a small cyan shard on the rim at day 220 and a dot at day 300. The thing that can kill me is the hardest thing to see.
- **By day 300 it's red cubes on red cubes.** I can't tell which regions are finished, which are still spreading, or where the firewall actually is. The firewall shrank from a dome to a cyan ring and a label.
- **The pink confetti cloud at the top-left of the globe** at days 220 and 300 reads as a glitch or an explosion. If those are the Automation drones, they're too small and too abstract to read. Same for the tiny red specks floating in space.
- **Round 5 fixes are gone from the HUD.** "2155 / 308" is two big bare numbers side by side, with no coin icon to tell me which one is money. The action bar is back to bare numbers, so "38d" sits next to "30" and I can't tell cooldown from price again. Tiles lost their effect chips. Dim tiles versus bright tiles is my only clue for affordable versus locked.
- **The cheerful chatbot is gone.** The red eye is cool and menacing, but the joke of the game (a *helpful* assistant ending the world) now rests entirely on two lines of copy, "Optimizing humanity." and "Almost done.".
- **No event or permission card appears in any frame,** so I can't judge how the decisions look in 3D.

verdict: The most screenshot-worthy frame so far and properly ominous, but as a strategy screen it hides half the board, loses the Kill Switch on the rim, and undid my round-5 HUD fixes.
strengths:
- Doom is visible without reading: the atmosphere goes from blue to red and the city lights go out. It's the best "numbers go up" feedback in any round.
- The firewall dome at day 220 is clear cause and effect. I can see the humans fight back exactly where they're doing it.
- The red HAL eye, the network arcs and the machine cubes make the AI read instant, with no words needed.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Keep the fight in view.** Auto-rotate or tilt the globe so the threatened region and Research Ridge are always on the visible face. Make the Kill Switch a cyan beam that shoots out into space, readable even from the rim, and growing taller with progress.
- **Add region chips on the globe.** Use 9 small floating pins with a percentage fill ring (no names at rest; names on hover), so I know which region is which and how close each one is.
- **Separate the reds by day 300.** Done regions go dark and dead (black cells, no lights). Spreading regions keep the bright red extruded cubes with cables. Keep the firewall as a dome right up until it fires, never just a ring.
- **Bring back the round-5 HUD language.** Use one coin icon on cycles, every tile price and every action price. Show cooldown as a pie sweep, not "38d" text. Put the main effect chip back on buyable tiles. Give owned, buyable, can't-afford and locked their own clear styles.
- **Make the drones read as drones:** small red quadcopter or satellite silhouettes orbiting in visible numbers, not a pink particle cloud.
- **Put HELPY's face back somewhere.** For example, the red eye could blink into the old pixel smile when an upgrade lands, or the permission card could pop out of a chat bubble. That way the menace carries the joke instead of replacing it.

## e2-war-table (diorama), day 60 / 220 / 300

**5-second gut:** I'd play it, but I'd hesitate before showing a friend. Day 220 is great: a huge cyan Kill Switch beam fires straight up out of Research Ridge, a cyan wall rises around Grain Expanse, red machine towers stand next to blue human buildings with white domes, and red arcs jump between continents. That's the most "I can see what's happening" frame of the round. But day 60 looks like a lavender toy city on an empty grid, with half the screen as blank table. The camera keeps cutting regions off behind the upgrade panel ("OLD C…", "RESEARCH RI…").

**What I'd do first / click:** I'd want to pan or zoom out to find the other regions, because I can only see two or three at a time. Then I'd buy upgrades and watch the towers turn red. I'd also hit the duck when the wall goes up.

**What confuses me:**
- **I can never see the whole board.** It's a strategy game with 9 regions and I see at most 3 per frame. Where are Petrol Peninsula, Factory Delta and Megalopolis Isles?
- **The upgrade panel sits on top of the map.** At days 220 and 300 the right-hand continent and its labels are hidden behind it.
- **Humans and HELPY are too close in colour at day 60.** The human towers are mauve-pink, the infected hexes are red and the cables are pink. I had to squint to tell mine from theirs. The amber-windowed buildings promised in the brief read as lavender here.
- **The region labels lie flat on the table in perspective.** They're skewed, cropped and some overlap the geometry ("GRAIN EXPANSE" runs through the hexes).
- **It has the same HUD regressions as E1:** no coin icon, bare action numbers, "39d" text cooldown, no effect chips. There's also the same pink confetti cloud, and no event or permission card in any frame.

verdict: The best "watch my moves play out" prototype, with the Kill Switch beam and firewall wall as real readable spectacle, but the camera crops the board and the HUD covers it, so it plays like a cutscene, not a strategy screen.
strengths:
- The Kill Switch beam and the firewall wall are huge, cyan and unmistakable. Human resistance has a physical shape you can't miss.
- Human towers turning into red machine towers is street-level cause and effect. You see HELPY replace people.
- The tabletop diorama is more distinctive than another glowing globe, and "You won't need these." on the eye logo is properly creepy.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Frame the whole board.** Use a default camera that shows all 9 regions at a steeper top-down angle, with nothing cropped. Zoom in only for an event, then return.
- **Clear the map.** Dock the upgrade grid as a collapsible tray, or shrink it to the 3 buyable tiles plus a "more" handle, so no region ever sits behind UI.
- **Fix the human vs machine colours.** Human buildings get warm amber windows on grey or blue. HELPY gets red only. Remove the mauve and pink from human towers.
- **Use readable labels.** Billboard the region labels (always face the camera, never skewed), put them on short stems above each region, and pair each with a percentage fill ring.
- **Fill the dead foreground.** At day 60 the bottom third is empty table. Tighten the camera or put the threat and countdown plate there.
- **Bring back the round-5 HUD language,** the same as for E1: coin icon everywhere, pie-sweep cooldowns, effect chips on buyable tiles.
