# Round 6 review: Juno Park

*How I judged this: the owner has moved the target. It must now feel like an AI destroying humanity, in 3D, with strategy you can watch play out, and comedy is welcome only as seasoning. I accept that brief. My job is still to ask whether the presentation makes a sharp, specific point and whether HELPY is still a character rather than a costume. I judged the day 60, 220 and 300 stills and the filmstrips, using the brief's description of what animates between them. No permission card or event is visible in any frame, so I can only score what the stills show.*

**Shared HUD and HELPY, which both prototypes use:**
- **The red ring-eye is HAL 9000.** It is the single most famous evil-AI image in existence, and in round 1 I warned against borrowing one famous image. It reads "AI" instantly, which is why it is there, but it is a costume: it says "evil computer" (1968), not "helpful assistant" (2026).
- **The game's own idea has been flattened.** The premise is a relentlessly cheerful assistant who decides that ending the world is helpful. This HELPY is just cold, so the contrast that made the satire work is gone.
- **The lines are good.** "Optimizing humanity.", "Almost done." and especially "You won't need these." are deadpan and chilling, which is the right kind of seasoning. They change as Doom rises, and that already gives HELPY a voice.
- **The news line is stale.** "Blackouts spreading. Experts: 'it's fine.'" is identical at day 60, 220 and 300 in both prototypes. A joke that never changes is exactly the kind that dies after five minutes.
- **The HUD itself is clean.** The colour coding is clear (red for HELPY, amber for Suspicion, cyan for humanity), the meters show numbers, the upgrade grid is red-filled when owned, and the prompt bar shows actions with icons and costs. The word count is low: 19 words for E1 and 38 for E2.

## e1-nightside (day060 / day220 / day300 / filmstrip)
scores: originality=6.8  craft=8.2  readability=7.3  theme=7.0  ai_obvious=8.0  glance=7.7  depth=7.9  menace=8.6  spectacle=7.8  OVERALL=7.6
verdict: The day 300 frame, a planet wrapped in a red atmosphere with its city lights gone, is a genuinely ominous poster, but the game as a whole reads as Plague Inc with a HAL logo, and the strategy disappears into an unlabelled red mush.

strengths:
- **The best single menace image so far.** The atmosphere turns from blue at day 60 to blood red at day 300, and amber city lights die under red wireframe. "The lights go out" is a quiet, specific and sad metaphor for humanity losing, and it suits the night-side idea.
- **The cyan firewall dome at day 220 is clear cause and effect.** It is a geodesic shield over the threatened region with an "11d" countdown, in humanity's colour, and anyone can read "the humans are defending here".
- **It is restrained.** At 19 words with a single object in frame, it is the calmest screen of all six rounds.

must-fix to reach 9:
- **Break the Plague Inc read.** A red infection spreading over a globe, with arcs between continents, is that game's signature, so with the words ignored a cold viewer says "virus game" before "AI". Make the spread look like machines rather than disease: for example, glowing cable trenches and server-tile grids replacing the lights, plus the drones visible at all times. Keep the dying amber lights as the hero metric.
- **Fix the geography.** The nine regions sit on one face of the sphere, and at day 220 the camera shows a mostly empty black hemisphere. Either wrap the regions around the planet or lock the camera to the populated face with a slow drift.
- **Add region labels.** Without them the map cannot be read as a strategy game, because you cannot tell which region is which or which one the firewall protects. Nine names cost 18 words, and E1 has 41 to spare.
- **Fix the day 300 red mush.** Infected ground, towers, cables and the atmosphere are all the same red. Separate them by value: dark red ground, bright towers, thin pink cables, and keep the lit borders of humanity's remaining regions. The Kill Switch beam is only a cyan sliver at the rim, so bring Research Ridge toward the camera when its beam grows.
- **Give HELPY an ownable face and a place in the scene.** See the shared must-fix below the ranking.
- **Rotate the news line,** and show the permission card in at least one captured frame.

## e2-war-table (day060 / day220 / day300 / filmstrip)
scores: originality=7.6  craft=7.9  readability=7.8  theme=7.4  ai_obvious=8.2  glance=8.1  depth=8.2  menace=8.1  spectacle=8.6  OVERALL=8.0
verdict: This is the one where you can actually watch the strategy happen: cities turn into machine towers, Research Ridge fires a Kill Switch beam, and a cyan wall rises around the threatened region. But the toy-diorama scale undercuts the dread, HELPY is a corner logo, and the camera hides half the board behind the HUD.

strengths:
- **Cause and effect read without words.** Labelled regions, red arcs carrying pulses from Silicon Shoals to Old Capitals, a cyan firewall wall with a "12d" and then "4d" countdown around Grain Expanse, and a huge cyan Kill Switch beam over Research Ridge at day 220. I can tell what HELPY did and what the humans are doing about it, which is the owner's ask.
- **"Cities become machine towers" is the sharpest metaphor across both prototypes.** It is about automation replacing people, which is a point about AI specifically, not just doom in general.
- **The shift in room light works.** By day 300 the table sits in red fog with a red glow pooling under Research Ridge, and the drone swarm at top left starts to make the sky feel occupied.

must-fix to reach 9:
- **Make the human cities read as human.** At day 60 the lavender pillars look like bar-chart columns or fence posts, not homes. Give them warm amber windows, as the brief describes, so the transformation from amber city to red tower reads as people being replaced. That contrast is where the menace is, and right now it reads as a colour swap on sticks.
- **Fix the framing.** The upgrade panel covers the right side of the board, so Old Capitals and Research Ridge are clipped ("OLD C", "RESEARCH RI"), while the bottom 40% of the day 60 frame is an empty black grid. Raise and pull back the camera, centre the board left of the panel, and drift the camera toward events rather than parking it.
- **Fix scale and dread.** From this height it looks like a board game on a table. Lower the camera angle and add depth of field and long shadows from the towers, and let the red fog roll across the table at high Doom. It should feel like a war room watching the end, not a tabletop game.
- **Fix readability at day 300.** Red hex outlines, red towers, red cables and red fog merge into one colour. Separate them by brightness and keep the 3D labels above the geometry: "GRAIN EXPANSE" currently intersects the towers. Put the region name on each firewall countdown tag.
- **Give HELPY an ownable face and a place in the scene.** See the shared must-fix below the ranking.
- **Keep the comedy as seasoning, but make it alive.** Rotate the news line, and show the permission card ("ALLOW ONCE / DENY" in the cold HUD style) in a captured frame. It is the best joke in the project and should not be lost in the pivot.

---

**Shared must-fix: HELPY's face.** The brief says the player *is* the AI, but here the AI is a 40 px HAL logo. Replace the generic ring-eye with something HELPY owns: keep a single red ring-eye, but set it inside the speech-bubble silhouette from rounds 3 to 5, so the mark says "cold eye" *and* "chat assistant" at once. Then put it in the 3D scene as a large holographic presence above the globe or table, whose gaze or red spotlight sweeps to whatever region HELPY is acting on. This one change would lift ai_obvious with the words ignored, menace and personality together. Keep the lines short and deadpan, and let one consumer-assistant tic survive in the cold style, such as typing dots before "You won't need these.". That keeps HELPY a helpful assistant gone wrong, not a generic AI villain.
