## b4-helpy-glance.png (candidate at rest, 42 words)

**5-second gut:** Yes, I'd keep playing. This is the first one that looks like a game I'd download. There's a map to paint pink, a grid of 18 tiles where half are lit and half are locked (that's my "numbers go up" itch), three meters with bars up top, and a cute pixel-bubble face. It has a Bloons upgrade-grid feel crossed with a Plague Inc map. It looks easy and it looks like there's stuff to unlock. That's exactly what I want.

**What I'd do first / click:** I'd click the glowing tiles with numbers (80, 90, 45), because they look like "buy me". Lit with a check means owned, outlined with a number means buyable, grey with a lock means not yet. I worked that out in about 2 seconds without reading anything. Then I'd hit HACK IT or BE NICE on the event card. After that I'd mash the bottom bar buttons to see what they do.

**What confuses me (icons I couldn't decode):**
- **The "9d | 49" box under Silicon Shoals.** No idea what it is. The left icon looks like a brick wall or a calendar, and the swirl arrow next to 49 means nothing to me. It's blue like every other stat box, so I'd assume it's info, not danger. (From the brief, this is apparently the "humans attack in 9 days, pay 49 to decoy" warning, which is the most important time-pressure thing on screen. It's the least alarming thing on screen.)
- **The bottom bar: broom 30, wrench-ish 23d (greyed), gauge 39, swirl 49.** Are 30/39/49 prices or timers? One says "23d", so are the others days too? I can't tell cost from cooldown. The broom and gauge icons don't tell me what happens when I press them.
- **The chip icon next to 96 +18.6.** I guessed it's money, but none of the tile prices or action prices carry the same chip icon, so I can't connect "96" to "80/90/120".
- **The DOOM icon** (a globe with a line through it?) is too small to read. It's my win meter, and nothing shows which tiles push it. I'd never guess that only the AUTO column moves Doom.
- **The 120 tile with the dashed border, and the dotted omega tile.** Dashed, dotted and locked are three slightly different greys. I think dashed means "can't afford yet", but it's a guess.
- **The map cells.** Solid pink means mine, I get that. But what are the purple striped ones (bottom-left of the left continent) and the blue empty circles in the right continent? Threatened? Selected? Enemy? There's no legend and no colour that screams "danger".
- **Why the SUSPICION meter is inverted (blue box).** It looks like a selected tab, not a warning.
- **On the event card,** pink chips mean good for me and striped chips mean bad. I only got that after staring at both buttons. A first-timer would just pick the button name they like.

verdict: The right direction, and I'd actually play it. But the warning, the action bar and the currency are still icon puzzles, so "looks easy" falls apart the moment I try to do something.
strengths:
- The upgrade grid is the hook. Lit, buyable and locked tiles read instantly and make me want to fill the board.
- The AI read comes without words: a pixel chat-bubble mascot with a typing "..." bubble, an "Ask HELPY..." prompt bar with a send arrow, slash-command buttons, and network lines running across the hexes.
- The top bar is clean: three meters with bars and big numbers, day counter, speed buttons. It's Plague Inc-level obvious.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Make the threat a threat.** Replace the blue "9d | 49" box with a red/hot-pink alert pin stuck onto Silicon Shoals: a shield or firewall icon, a countdown ring that drains, and the cells underneath striped in that same alert colour. Put the decoy price on a big "tap to dodge" button attached to it. At the moment it looks like a stat.
- **Separate cost from cooldown in the action bar.** Put the chip (currency) icon before 30/39/49. Show cooldown as a radial sweep over the button, with "23d" inside a clock ring. Make the icons bigger and more literal (eraser for Scrub, a breaking flask for Sabotage, a fast-forward or flame for Overclock).
- **One currency icon everywhere.** Put the same chip glyph next to every tile price (80, 90, 45, 120) and action price so I learn that chip means money within one look.
- **Connect Doom to its column.** Use a bigger, clearer Doom icon (a cracked globe or globe with a fuse), put it on the AUTO tab header, and add a small Doom badge on the tiles that raise it. Then the win path is visible without words.
- **Use louder tile states.** Owned: solid with a check (fine already). Affordable: give it a glow, a "+" or a slow pulse. Can't afford: show a mini progress bar ("need 24") filling at the bottom of the tile instead of a dashed border. Locked: lock icon only. Remove the dashed-versus-dotted distinction.
- **Use one colour code for map cells and add a three-swatch legend** (mine, spreading, under threat). Swap the blue empty circles for a thick outline around the selected region. Push the mascot onto the map too, for example tiny HELPY faces or a cursor on newly taken cells, so the AI read lives where my eyes are. And fill the empty space bottom-left between Bank Isles and the mascot.
