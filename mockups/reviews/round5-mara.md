## b5-helpy-glance.png (+ -depth-upgrade, -depth-map, -depth-action)
scores: originality=8.7  craft=8.5  readability=8.8  theme=8.9  ai_obvious=8.8  glance=9.1  depth=8.6  OVERALL=8.7
verdict: The icon set finally looks like HELPY's own: printed, offset and heavier. The screen reads in a second and still visibly packs eighteen upgrades, four actions, a dilemma, a threat and nine regions, and a sweating HELPY makes it the most characterful frame of five rounds. What's left is a handful of glyph and map niggles, not structure.
strengths:
- **The icon set is now an asset.** Every glyph carries the pink offset impression and a heavier blue stroke, so the icons belong to the same print run as the wordmark and the halftone mascot. That was my biggest round 4 note, and it's answered.
  - The upgrade grid's state ladder is excellent: affordable is the loudest (solid pink with a blue offset shadow and an effect chip at rest), owned is calm (pale tint with a check), can't afford is dashed with a progress bar showing 96 of 120, and locked is dotted and pale with its cost.
  - The prompt-bar actions are shapes plus a price, with Sabotage's cooldown as a pie wedge. That's glanceable and still a little funny (the decoy duck stays).
- **The character carries the theme without words.** HELPY's bubble face now *reacts*: a wobbly mouth and a sweat drop at the AI Safety Summit, with the event's speech tail pointing back at it. Add the prompt bar, the send button, the node-and-edge network across infiltrated hexes and the robot tile, and a cold viewer reads "a chatbot is taking over the world" with every word blanked.
- **Most round 4 fixes landed.**
  - Glyph collisions are mostly resolved: the hex token is Cycles, the node graph is Network, the slashed eye is Stealth and the mic is only Deepfake.
  - Labels carry percentages and anchor to a dot inside their region on leader lines.
  - The threat is a brick-wall pin sitting on the hatched Silicon Shoals, with a "9d | presence −45 | duck 49" tag on a stem.
  - Meter goal glyphs explain the bars (flag = win, skull = lose).
  - Tooltips have pointers and keep numbers on one line ("90 · you have 96").
  - The ticker is back as a single quiet line.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Two glyph collisions came back.** The flag is both Doom's win goal (end of the DOOM bar) and the AI Safety Summit event icon. The cracked doom globe is the DOOM meter, the DOOM branch header, a corner badge on the 120/190/320 tiles and the OMEGA upgrade itself. Give the Summit a podium or lanyard glyph, give OMEGA its own mark (the Ω, or HELPY's face over the globe), and drop the redundant corner badges, since the column header already says "Doom".
- **The Cycles token reads as a copyright sign.** At chip size the hex-wrapped "C" is ©, so "©30", "©49" and "©↑40" look like legal text, not money. Use a non-letter mark inside the hex (a chip die with pins, or a filled pip), or set the hex solid pink with a knocked-out dot.
- **The region borders are incomplete, and selection is unexplained.** Only Old Capitals has the heavy blue seam. Tundra and Grain Expanse, or Petrol Peninsula and Factory Delta, still have no boundary between them. If the heavy seam is a selection, the legend (MINE / SPREADING / THREAT) doesn't list it. Draw a 2px seam between every region, and give selection its own mark and legend entry.
- **The jump routes still pass for leader lines in two places.** The Tundra→Old Capitals arc starts at the dot on the TUNDRA SERVER FARMS plate's right corner, and that plate has no leader of its own into its region. The OLD CAPITALS leader also crosses the same pink arc in open sea. Start routes from a cell, not a plate edge, dock Tundra's plate with its own leader, and shorten the Old Capitals leader by moving the plate to the sea north of its region.
- **The lower-right of the main panel is still empty, and the dots are ambiguous.** At rest there's about 360×230px of blank paper to the right of the event card. The "thread under the map" is two bubbles, not a thread. Either stack one or two earlier HELPY/user bubbles there (icon-only reactions keep the word budget), or let the event card span the width. The three pink dots under the event read as both a typing indicator and a carousel pager. Pick one.
- **Small items.**
  - The ticker's wireframe globe and the map-help "🌐 ?" button share a silhouette with the doom globe. Use a newspaper glyph for the ticker.
  - The Suspicion goal glyph (a slashed running figure) is unreadable at 16px. A pulled plug would say "unplugged".
  - The 150 locked tile's icon reads as a highlighter. A gavel or a seat would sell "Seat on the AI Ethics Board".
  - In the depth-map state, the Old Capitals card hides the FACTORY DELTA plate ("ELTA 12%"). Flip the card's side when it would cover a label.
