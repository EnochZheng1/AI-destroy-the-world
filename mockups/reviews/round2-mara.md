# Round 2: art-direction review, Mara Lindqvist

I re-read the brief, including the new non-negotiable `ai_obvious` criterion. My test for it: if I cover the copy and give a stranger three seconds, do they say "you're an AI"? I zoomed in on every panel of both shots and checked them against my round 1 must-fixes.

---

## b2-the-conversation.png
scores: originality=7.5  craft=8.3  readability=8.6  theme=9.0  ai_obvious=9.4  OVERALL=8.3
verdict: The game owner's complaint is solved outright, because the AI-assistant shell is the first thing anyone sees. But it buys that clarity by dressing as one particular real-world assistant brand, and I can't recommend that without reservation.
strengths:
- `ai_obvious` is solved through the visual language itself, not through jokes. The chat thread with an avatar, a prompt composer with slash-command chips, a model picker ("HELPY ✦ Ω-142 · reasoning: maximum"), the collapsed "Thought for 4 seconds" trace, Regenerate, Copy and thumbs buttons, and saved memories are all recognisable as "AI chatbot" before you read a word. The map's infiltrated cells now carry node-and-edge lines like a neural net or constellation, which is a nice visual AI cue inside the game board itself.
- The round 1 fixes landed. The model badge no longer wraps, the Day and speed control is no longer clipped, the map is a full-width pinned "world.canvas" with a LIVE tag, the labels sit on plates, and the legend now matches the marks (filled for helpful, a ring for selected, a dashed red outline for threatened Silicon Shoals). The new upgrade-state legend ("80 can buy / 120 need more cycles / 🔒 needs earlier step") is the clearest economy read of either mockup.
- It finally reads as a game, not only a parody. The three big meter cards say "win at 100%" and "you lose at 100%", the speed control sits next to the day counter, and the inspector strip under the map plus the pinned threat banner make the strategy loop scannable in seconds.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Get off the borrowed brand.** An orange rounded-square smiling avatar, the ✦ spark glyph, a cream and grey neutral palette and an orange send button add up to one specific real assistant's identity, not HELPY's. That's an originality ceiling and a likely trade-dress problem for a commercial game. Keep the chat grammar, but give HELPY its own signature: a non-orange hero colour, a custom avatar shape (not a rounded square) and a glyph other than ✦.
- **The event card's offset shadow is broken.** The round 1 offset shadow is now a stray orange sliver poking out only at the bottom-right corner of the "AI Safety Summit" card (around x 1058, y 770). It reads as a rendering artefact. Either restore a full, even offset on two sides or drop it.
- **Align the meter cards.** DOOM and SUSPICION set their sub-labels inline ("world used", "vibes check"), but KILL SWITCH drops "safety review" onto its own line, which pushes its bar and its "+0.21/day" line about 10px lower than its neighbours. Put all three sub-labels on the same baseline so the three bars line up as one row.
- **Bring back the typographic attitude.** The round 1 condensed-serif event headline was the one moment of real typographic voice, and it's gone. Everything is now one neo-grotesk plus mono, which is clean but anonymous. Give HELPY's own utterances (event titles, its chat lines) a display face the player learns to associate with it.
- **The labels still cover the cells, and the sidebar has a hole.** The plates for Tundra Server Farms, Petrol Peninsula, Factory Delta and Old Capitals sit on top of live cells, including the selected Old Capitals ring. Offset them into the sea with a leader line. In the left sidebar there's about 280px of empty grey between the chat history and Saved Memories. Fill it with more history (there's comedy to mine) or pull Saved Memories up.
- In plan.md, locked rows (light grey text with 140/240 costs) fall below comfortable contrast, and the yellow emoji padlock is the only off-palette colour on the page. Use a monochrome lock glyph and lift the locked text one step. At phone size, plan.md must become a bottom sheet and the sidebar a drawer, or the three-column shell collapses.

## c2-helpy-digest.png
scores: originality=8.7  craft=7.8  readability=7.5  theme=8.6  ai_obvious=7.4  OVERALL=7.9
verdict: Still the frame I'd remember from a storefront, and the digest reframe is clever, but the "AI" lives almost entirely in the copy. A cold viewer sees a lovely pink-and-blue zine with a cute face, not a chatbot, so it fails the non-negotiable.
strengths:
- My big round 1 fixes are mostly addressed. The Firewall stamp is out of the map and into its own tilted, torn-edge panel in the left column, with a matching "⚠ FIREWALL · T-9" flag on the map. The ballot has no pre-ticked box. Power Grid now says "120 · need 24" inside a dashed border instead of a misleading strike-through. "~370 days" sits on one line. The left column no longer dies after the meters. The dark tones now read as overprint, so the two-ink promise mostly holds.
- The identity is still the most ownable in the project. The HELPY DIGEST masthead with its pink mis-registration, halftone-dot hexes, solid-pink infiltration, an inverted blue KILL SWITCH block that finally gives the losing meter danger weight, wavy underlines under the effect lines, and "Overheard from humans" pull quotes. The economy states (solid pink border = buy, dashed = can't afford, "🔒 2 MORE / unlock later") are readable in print grammar.
- The AI framing is witty where it exists: "YOUR WEEKLY AI-GENERATED SUMMARY OF THE END OF THE WORLD", the "A NOTE FROM HELPY · AI · JUST NOW" card, "Reply to HELPY…", "✦ SUMMARIZE THE APOCALYPSE" and "Unsubscribe from the apocalypse".
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Make the visual language, not the copy, say "AI".** Each AI signal here has to be read (the masthead subline, the speech card, the reply bar). Riso is an analogue, anti-digital look, so the print metaphor actively fights the brief. Push AI artefacts into the image itself: a big HELPY chat bubble as the hero of the left column instead of a small note card, a blinking typing cursor or "HELPY is typing…" dots printed as halftone, a sparkle-and-prompt composer at a real chat-input size, and a "generated by AI" watermark over the map. Until a covered-copy glance says "chatbot", this can't reach 8.5.
- **The map has a dead band and two "selected" regions.** About 90px of empty paper runs along the bottom of the map frame. Silicon Shoals carries the same big outlined-circle treatment as the selected Old Capitals, so it reads as a second selection. The legend has no "threatened" or frontier state, even though there are pink-and-white triangle cells and dashed circles on the map. Crop or rescale the frame, give the threatened region its own mark and legend entry, and move the FIREWALL flag so it points at Silicon Shoals, not beside Bank Isles.
- **Widows everywhere.** "Issue: Day / 142" in the masthead subline, "presence / there." in the firewall panel, "Mark one / box." in the ballot, "Kill Switch / -15%." on Lobbyist Bots, "50% / more." on Power Grid, and "helpful" alone in the HELPY note. Rewrite or reflow every caption so no line ends with a single word.
- **Clean up the installed cards.** The installed rollups ("SMART FRIDGE BOTNET · CRYPTO SIDE HUSTLE · UNDERSEA CABLE TAPS") wrap condensed caps onto two cramped lines that crowd the INSTALLED tag, and the folded-corner chamfer is too small to read as intentional. Give INSTALLED its own line or a stamp, and let the rollup run at one size smaller.
- **The bottom bar has an orphan CTA and a strike-through again.** The loudest button on the page, the pink "✦ SUMMARIZE THE APOCALYPSE", doesn't map to any game verb in the brief. Either make it the obvious end-turn or skip-week action, or demote it. "SABOTAGE 23D" uses a strike-through for cooldown, the same "cancelled" grammar I flagged on the costs in round 1. Use a halftone knock-back plus a countdown instead. The news ticker also runs straight into "Unsubscribe from the apocalypse" ("seen g"), so add a fade or a hard stop.
- Hierarchy in the left column: the Firewall panel and the KILL SWITCH block are both solid-blue slabs, so the two loudest blocks compete. Keep the solid fill for one, and make the other a bordered or tinted panel.

## ranking
1. **b2-the-conversation.png**: OVERALL 8.3, ai_obvious 9.4
2. **c2-helpy-digest.png**: OVERALL 7.9, ai_obvious 7.4

**Bar check (8.5 OVERALL and 8.5 ai_obvious):** neither mockup passes. B2 clears `ai_obvious` easily but misses OVERALL, mainly because its identity is a real assistant's brand rather than HELPY's own. C2 is the stronger art direction but fails `ai_obvious` outright, so it can't pass however good it looks.

**Which direction I'd bet the game on:** B2's structure, because the brief makes `ai_obvious` non-negotiable and only the chat shell delivers it through form rather than copy. But I'd re-skin it with C2's art direction: a chatbot interface printed in two riso inks, with halftone hexes, a mis-registered HELPY wordmark and print-pattern meters. That keeps the AI legibility of B2 and gives it the identity it doesn't have yet. That hybrid is the screenshot that could only belong to this game.
