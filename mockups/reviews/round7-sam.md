## f1-nightside-helpy (day 60 / 220 / 230-summit / 300, plus 2 hover frames)

**5-second gut:** Yes and yes. I'd play it, and I'd send the day-220 frame to a friend with no caption. A giant red chat-bubble smiley floats over a planet like a hologram and shines a scan cone down onto it, while red machine cubes eat the continents, a cyan dome goes up over one region and a cyan beam fires out of another. That's the whole pitch in one picture: a cheerful assistant ending the world. It's cute and it's dread at the same time. The smiley is what makes it, because without it this would be another evil-red-eye game. By day 300 the atmosphere is blood red, finished regions have gone dark, and the only bright things left are the firewall dome and HELPY's face. That reads as "almost over" with zero words.

**What I'd do first / click:** I'd look at the left strip, because its rings fill up like a progress list and Grain is already at 47%. Then I'd watch the tiles whose red "need" bars are filling, and buy the first one that's ready. When the dome showed up, I'd hit the red duck-⬡50 button on the threat chip, because it's the only red-filled button on the globe and it matches the duck in the action bar. On the summit card, I'd read the green and red chips and pick in about 2 seconds.

**What I can decode now (round-5 and round-6 fixes that landed):**
- The ⬡ coin appears on cycles, every tile and every action, so prices are obvious. The pie sweep on "39d" reads as cooldown, so I no longer mix up price and timer.
- The threat chip ("ring · 12d · ⬡45% · duck ⬡50") sits on the region, and Grain's row in the strip lights up cyan with a firewall icon. The danger finally has its own colour and its own spot.
- On the permission card, ALLOW ONCE and DENY each have eye and power chips with green or red arrows. Green means good for me. I got it the first time.
- The "/" prefixes are back on the actions, and so is the "ask HELPY..." prompt. The chatbot read is everywhere.
- The drones are now pink quadcopter X shapes at day 60. Big enough to read as "machines in the sky".

**What still confuses me:**
- **The Kill Switch beam changes shape depending on the camera.** At day 220 it's a sideways cyan lightsaber poking off the right rim. In the region hover frame, where Research Ridge faces the camera, it's a glowing cyan blob. At day 300 it's mostly **behind the upgrade panel**. The thing that can make me lose is still the hardest thing to track, and I can't see it get bigger between 11% and 28%.
- **The upgrade hover card has no effect chips.** "QUANTUM OVERCLOCK · ⬡240 · you have 29 · flavour line" tells me the price and a joke, but not what it does. B5's Lobbyist Bots card led with "👁↓25% ⏻↓15%". That pattern is missing here.
- **The owned tiles are the loudest thing in the grid.** Mid-game, 13 or 14 of the 18 tiles are owned, and their filled dark red pulls my eye more than the 3 or 4 I'm saving for. No frame shows a tile I can afford right now, so I can't confirm "buyable is loudest".
- **The globe pins are tiny unlabelled numbers** (82, 24, 6...). I can't tell which pin belongs to which row in the strip without hovering.
- **The pink particle cloud at the top-left of the globe is still there** (days 220, 230 and 300), along with some translucent red sheets near the top. Is it a shockwave? A drone swarm? It reads as a rendering glitch.
- **The loss chip "⬡45%"** uses a hex icon close to the coin. The % sign saves it, but it's still the second hex-shaped thing on the chip next to "⬡50".

verdict: The first frame across seven rounds that makes me want to play and to share. A smiling chat-bubble hologram slowly turning a planet red is the game in one picture, and the strategy HUD around it finally decodes without reading.
strengths:
- The hologram smiley over the globe, with its scan cone, is the AI read and the satire at once: cheerful, huge and clearly doing this on purpose. ai_obvious doesn't need a single word.
- Doom is the planet: the blue-to-red atmosphere, dark dead regions and a bright front make "how close am I" readable from across the room.
- The threat finally works as a system you can see: the cyan dome on the region, the countdown ring, the loss chip, a big red dodge button, and a matching highlighted row in the strip.
must-fix to reach 9:
- **Keep the Kill Switch beam readable from every angle.** Draw it straight up in screen space from Research Ridge, whatever the globe's rotation. Scale its height and brightness with the Kill Switch %, and never let it pass behind the upgrade panel. If the camera can't avoid that, pin a small "beam" glyph with its % at the panel edge.
- **Put effect chips first on the upgrade hover card,** for example: [effect chip] [effect chip] · ⬡240 · you have 29 · flavour. Use the same pattern on the region card, where "⬡2/day at 100%" should be a chip, not a sentence.
- **Make owned tiles recede.** Use a flat dim tint with a check and no red fill, and make the next tile I can afford the brightest thing in the grid (solid red, raised, effect chip, a pulse when it becomes affordable).
- **Link the pins to the strip.** Hovering a strip row should pulse that region and its pin on the globe, and the reverse. Make the pins about 1.5× larger with the same ring style as the strip, so the two read as one system.
- **Remove or explain the pink particle cloud and the floating red sheets.** If they're the Automation swarm, use the quadcopter shapes, which already work.
- **Tie the permission card to the world.** Draw a thin line from the card to the region it affects, and let HELPY's hologram turn toward that region, so the decision and its place on the globe connect. Give the loss chip a non-hex icon, such as a cracked cell, so ⬡ only ever means money.

**Phone concern:** The left strip, the globe and the right grid won't fit side by side at phone width. The strip could collapse into a horizontal row of 9 rings, and the grid into a bottom drawer. The globe itself works well in portrait.
